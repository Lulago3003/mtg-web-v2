Home / Impact indicators

If your cargo took a hit, you'll know.

Adhesive labels with a mechanical indicator that turns red, permanently, when the package takes an impact above its level. For fragile, electronic, medical and industrial cargo.

Get a quote on WhatsApp Request the PDF catalog

How it works

  1. Stick it on the box

    The label goes on the outside of the box before shipping. No tools or setup.

  2. Travels without triggering

    Normal transport vibration doesn't trigger it, only impacts above its level.

  3. If hit, it turns red

    The indicator turns red and can't be reset. That's the evidence for the claim.

Seven sensitivity levels

The lower the G-force, the more sensitive the label. We help you choose based on what you ship.

  • 5GPink

    Medical, lab and delicate electronics

  • 10GCyan

    Fragile electronics and servers

  • 15GBlue

    Instruments and sensitive equipment

  • 25GYellow

    Industrial and office equipment

  • 37GPurple

    Heavy equipment and appliances

  • 50GOrange

    Heavy machinery and motors

  • 75G+Black

    Extreme loads and heavy industry

What it solves and what it doesn't

So you can decide with all the facts.

What it solves

No batteries, no electronics
Passive mechanism: it doesn't run down and needs no setup or app.
Can't be reset
Once triggered it can't be tampered with. It's evidence for the claim.
On in seconds
Peel and stick, no tools or training.
Deterrent effect
Handlers are more careful when they see the label.
Low cost per unit
It costs little compared to an unclaimed damage.

What to keep in mind

Only detects impacts
It doesn't measure temperature, humidity or tilt.
Single use
Each shipment needs a new label.
Doesn't say who or where
It shows there was an impact, not when or where along the route.
The level must be right
Too low a level triggers on normal vibration; too high misses real damage.
Check before signing
If the receiver signs without checking the label, it loses value as proof. It doesn't replace insurance either.
